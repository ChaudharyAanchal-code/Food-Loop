BE A RESPONSIBLE DONOR

Good food deserves good care.

  • Donate only safe, untouched surplus food.
  • Use clean, food-grade, covered packaging.
  • Keep hot and cold food at suitable temperatures.
  • Clearly share preparation and last-consumption times.
  • Arrange pickup early enough for safe consumption.
⚠ FoodLoop is a student demonstration platform. Listings are not independently inspected or certified.
